# User Guide

This guide provides an overview of how to use the Readeck mobile app.

## Getting Started

1. **Install the App**: Install the app on your Android or iOS device.
2. **Login**:
   - Open the app.
   - Enter your Readeck server URL (e.g., `https://read.example.com`).
   - Enter your username and password.
   - Tap "Login".

## Features

### Managing Bookmarks

- **Browse**: The main screen displays your list of bookmarks. You can scroll through them to see your saved content.
- **Read**: Tap on any bookmark to open the detailed reader view. This allows you to read the content comfortably on your device.
- **Edit/Delete**: In the detailed view or via long-press (depending on implementation), you can edit bookmark details or delete a bookmark.

### Organization

Organize your reading list to keep things tidy:

- **Labels**: Apply labels to your bookmarks for easy categorization.
- **Collections**: Group related bookmarks into collections.
- **Navigation**: Use the bottom navigation or side drawer to access Labels and Collections screens specifically.

### Advanced Search

Find exactly what you're looking for:

1. Navigate to the Search screen.
2. Enter keywords.
3. Use filters to narrow down results by:
   - Labels
   - Collections
   - Date added

### Sharing (Adding Bookmarks)

You can save content from other apps directly to Readeck:

1. Open a web browser or another app (like Twitter/X, News apps).
2. Find an article or page you want to save.
3. Tap the **Share** button in that app.
4. Select **Readeck** from the list of apps.
5. The Readeck app will open briefly, show a loading animation, save the content, and then automatically close.
   - If saving fails, an error message will be displayed.

### Settings

Access the Settings screen to customize your experience:

- **Theme**: Switch between Light and Dark modes.
- **Account**: Manage your login session.
- **Data**: Manage offline cache and other data settings.
